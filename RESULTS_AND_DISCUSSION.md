# Results and Discussion

## Results

We used answers from 8 participants. All 8 chose AI B (Distant AI) when asked which AI they would like to work with again.

Distant AI got higher average ratings in all five questions:

| Question | Close AI | Distant AI |
|---|---:|---:|
| Enjoyment | 4.25 | 5.50 |
| Competence | 4.50 | 6.13 |
| Effectiveness | 4.88 | 6.38 |
| Coordination | 2.63 | 4.88 |
| Adaptation | 3.38 | 5.63 |

We also used a Wilcoxon test to compare each person's ratings for the two AIs. Some results had p-values below 0.05, but none were below 0.01 after correcting for the five tests. So we should be careful about claiming a clear statistical difference.

We checked how long it took to finish cars. The average was 20.48 seconds with Close AI and 19.16 seconds with Distant AI. The difference was about 1.32 seconds. The paired t-test gave p = 0.190, and the Wilcoxon test gave p = 0.148. We did not find enough evidence that one AI finished cars faster.

## Discussion

The players in our study seemed to like Distant AI more. One possible reason is that it worked more independently and did not get in the player's way as much. This could make teamwork feel easier.

The car completion times were quite similar. This is interesting because the players preferred Distant AI even though we did not find a clear speed difference. It looks like players may care about how the AI works with them, not just how fast it completes tasks.

We need to be careful with these results because only 8 people took part. We also tested several survey questions, and none of those comparisons passed the corrected significance threshold. A larger test could help us understand the difference better.
